The Particular Baptist tradition is rich with primary sources — confessions, catechisms, sermons, and theological treatises — many of which are now freely available online. The following is a curated collection of those resources, organized by category. I have gathered these from various digital archives and added some of my own. May they serve your study of this faithful stream of Reformed Christianity.

---

## Archive Collections

These are the major repositories where most of the primary sources below can be found.

- [Angus Library Treasures](http://theangus.rpc.ox.ac.uk/angus-treasures/) — described as the world's best archive of English Baptist history, held at Regent's Park College, Oxford
- [Transcribed Particular Baptist Works](http://www.vor.org/rbdisk/other.htm) — Voice of the Reformation's curated transcriptions
- [Particular Baptist Library](http://particularbaptistlibrary.org/authors.html) — an online library organized by author
- [Post Reformation Digital Library](http://www.prdl.org/) — broad Reformed primary source database
- [Baptist History and Heritage](https://baptisthistoryandthought.wordpress.com/) — essays and resources on Baptist history and thought
- [Broadmead Bristol Church Records, 1640–1687](https://archive.org/details/therecordsofachu00terruoft) — firsthand records of one of the earliest Particular Baptist congregations
