---

- Use along with a Bible reading calendar. If you use McCheyne, then only read the Psalms one time in McCheyne and use this calendar for the second reading. If using a different Bible reading calendar, you may substitute this reading for that one.
- Do not look up unknown words, but use an interlinear Bible or an English version. I prefer the American Standard or the New American Standard, because they will be more literal.
- Meditate and pray through the Psalm portion read.
- Make a catalogue of various worship helps and prayers found in the Psalms: names, titles, and attributes of God; various types of prayer (praise, thanksgiving, confession, lament, and petition); perhaps catalogue the various types of Psalms.
- When reading a Psalm over a period of days, review previous section(s) as you begin the new one. If you have time on the Lord's Day, review the Psalms read that week.
